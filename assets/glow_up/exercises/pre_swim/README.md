## Locked behavior

- Women-only exercise assets. Do not restore male models, gender selectors, or male asset fallbacks.
- Left/right and alternating exercises must complete every required side before advancing.
- A frame sequence is instructional media; do not synthesize missing body positions or interpolate impossible movements.
- Sharp pain, dizziness, numbness, or worsening discomfort must stop the session and show safe guidance.
- Session completion and discomfort feedback emit ecosystem events for the Glow Up Brain, journal, progress, and future plan adjustment.
